# Consent template — replace before use

> This placeholder is not valid consent language and must not be used to recruit participants. Have your institution or ethics committee review a study-specific statement before collecting data.

Before a real study, explain in plain language:

- the study purpose and what participation involves;
- foreseeable risks, benefits, and time commitment;
- what data will be collected, why, who can access it, and how long it will be kept;
- whether participation is voluntary and how to withdraw;
- contact details for the research team and independent complaints contact; and
- the required ethics approval details.

Replace this whole page with the approved participant information and consent process for your study.
